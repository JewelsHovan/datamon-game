## G-27: Per-move answer text for a 74-special roster

### Findings
- Smash Ultimate tips are tagged by fighter and move and come in three tiers. A fresh save shows only beginner tips, middle tips appear after 200 tips viewed, and high tips after 650 [primary, fan wiki] (https://www.ssbwiki.com/List_of_tips_(SSBU)).
- Fighter tips are one or two sentences, about 15 to 40 words. The fighters read had about 14 to 22 tips each. Example, Kirby's Inhale (beginner tier): "If you press the attack button after sucking enemies in, you'll spit them back out." Most special-move tips explain how to use the move, not how to beat it [primary, fan wiki, first quarter of the page read] (https://www.ssbwiki.com/List_of_tips_(SSBU)/Fighters).
- Fantasy Strike's official guide has no per-move or per-character counter text. It points players to character spotlight videos and practice mode, and teaches the yomi counter as a general rule [primary] (https://www.fantasystrike.com/guide).
- SF6's in-game Character Guide (Fighting Ground, Training) and Capcom's short per-character videos outline each character's key tools and "ideal situations". No source says they teach how to beat a specific move [secondary] (https://www.siliconera.com/new-street-fighter-6-how-to-play-videos-focus-on-juri-and-guile/, https://filmstories.co.uk/gaming/street-fighter-6-character-guide/, snippets).
- Tekken 8's Arcade Quest "recommends in game tips" and basic combos, step by step. How it words a counter to a move: not found [secondary] (https://www.siliconera.com/a-beginners-guide-to-excelling-in-tekken-8/, snippet).
- No shipped game was found that gives a one-line answer to every special move. Tekken 8's general replay tip (2026-11-15 note, Dengeki) is still the closest precedent.

### Candidate additions
- A-022 (new): the versus splash shows one answer line for the opponent's signature special, taken from A-021's per-move answer table. Smash shows its tips while a match loads, and its tiers suggest an order: the basic answer first, finer ones once a player has seen the basics.
- A-021 (updated): the per-move answer table should hold one line of about 15 words per special, after Smash's tip length. A second, finer line can be added later. No rescore.
- Dropped: per-character videos (SF6, Fantasy Strike). The game can't make 37 videos cheaply, and video gives the player nothing to act on during the next match.

## G-25: Comeback mechanics that spare newcomers a perfect K.O.

### Findings
- Killer Instinct (2013) producer Torin Retting: the team wanted a mechanic that "helped facilitate come backs from tough situation", and "As opposed to Shadow Meter, you build Instinct Meter by taking damage and doing Combo Breakers" [primary] (https://www.siliconera.com/making-new-killer-instinct-everything-combo-breakers-selecting-characters/).
- Infil's KI guide: Instinct "fills up as you take damage and perform combo breakers", and "you'll earn your first instinct no later than around 30% health left on your first health bar". It lasts 15 seconds and "does not dominate the game to the extent Marvel vs. Capcom 3's X-Factor does" [primary] (https://ki.infil.net/meter2.html).
- GBVS Rising creative director Tetsuya Fukuhara: "we received a lot of feedback that defense is too strong, which makes players prone to stalemate", and "we want both advanced and beginner players alike to use Raging Strikes to break their foe's guard at critical moments" [primary] (https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/20230901-21299/, read through the site's JSON feed).
- Mortal Kombat 1 Kameos (Ed Boon): "It's just the push of a button and you get this free move", and "we really wanted a more simple, straightforward approach" than tag teams. The article's "turn the tide" wording is the writer's, not Boon's [primary] (https://gamedeveloper.com/design/why-netherrealm-went-with-a-kameo-mechanic-for-mortal-kombat-1).
- SFV V-Gauge builds mainly as your character takes damage and doesn't carry over between rounds. Peter Rosas warned SFIV players not to treat it as a comeback mechanic and wait to use it [secondary] (https://www.pcgamer.com/street-fighter-5-introduces-the-v-gauge, page body didn't load; https://gamesbeat.com/street-fighter-v-round-3-yoshinori-ono-dishes-on-the-comeback-mechanic/, read 2026-11-14).

### Candidate additions
- A-018 (updated): adds a second, concrete target from KI. A player who is losing has their super by about 30% health left, whatever the opponent does. The headless check reports both numbers: the median frame the bar first fills, and the health at which the losing side first has a full bar. Torin Retting's damage-fed rationale [primary] backs the taking-damage gain. No rescore.
- Dropped: per-character comeback modes (KI Instinct). That's 37 more mechanics, and the battlemon domains already give each fighter's super its own behavior.
- Dropped as a new addition: a guard-breaking super (GBVSR Raging Strike). It's worth checking whether the battlemon supers break a turtling defender, but that's a step 7 question for G-28, not yet a spec-ready addition.
