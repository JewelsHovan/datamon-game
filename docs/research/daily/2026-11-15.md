## G-24: Replay analysis heuristics

### Findings
- Dengeki Online hands-on and interview: Tekken 8's replay advice comes in two kinds. General tips work for every character, for example "この技は二段目が上段なので、しゃがめば反撃できます" ("this move's second hit is high, so you can crouch and counter"). Matchup tips depend on the characters, for example "このシーン、ガード後にこの技で反撃できました" ("in this scene, you could have countered with this move after blocking"). When a better move was available, the replay pauses briefly and shows the advice as a caption [primary] (https://dengekionline.com/articles/213882).
- Same interview: director Kohei Ikeda (池田幸平) wanted to review his own losses from replay data. He built the feature to teach combos (offense) and guaranteed punishes (defense) straight from replays, because Tekken 7's similar coaching made players go into practice mode first. He calls the advice "アーケードスタイルで遊ぶ中上級者向けの機能" ("a feature for intermediate and advanced players who play Arcade Style"). It shows Arcade Style commands, not the simplified Special Style ones [primary] (same URL).
- Same interview: the producer said Special Style punish tips would be considered if enough players asked for them [primary] (same URL).
- How Tekken 8 decides a punish was missed: not found. No official page, patch note or guide explains it. Guides say only that a tip appears "if there is one available" when the player pauses [primary] (https://www.downtimebros.com/tekken-8-how-to-practice-with-tips-on/).
- Tekken 7's version was described as showing "punish chances that were missed", added in a free update rather than in Season 2 [secondary] (search summary, https://gamingtrend.com/news/haradas-gonna-beat-you-up-and-youre-gonna-practice-tekken-7-gets-new-practice-mechanics-for-dlc).
- SF6: no official replay feature that analyzes punishes was found. A community-made match-analysis tool exists [secondary] (https://dashfight.com/news/street-fighter-6-match-analysis-tool-5021, fetch timed out). A replay take-over modeled on Tekken was reported as planned, but whether it shipped is not confirmed [secondary] (mxdwn, fetch timed out).
- GameSkinny and Prima Games returned 403.

### Candidate additions
- A-020 (updated): Tekken's own director aims replay tips at intermediate players using the full command set, and Tekken 8's simplified-input players don't get punish tips. That weakens the newcomer case for missed-punish detection. The newcomer-friendly kind is the general tip ("crouch the second hit"), which is a per-move answer. Rescoped: the first version pauses only on moments the per-move answer table (shared with A-021) covers. Missed-punish cards come second, using a rule this game can compute: the defender blocked a move whose remaining recovery was longer than the defender's fastest startup, and didn't hit back in that window (design inference, unsourced). No rescore: the narrower scope offsets the weaker evidence.
- A-021 (updated): Tekken 8's general tip type ("this move's second hit is high, so crouch") is the same thing as A-021's per-move answer line [primary]. No rescore.
- Dropped: showing tips in full command notation. This game has no motion inputs, so every answer is already one direction plus one button.

## G-22: Summon counterplay

### Findings
- Smash Ultimate: Ultimate made "nearly all" Assist Trophies that attack fighters directly damageable. They smoke at 50% HP and glow at 20%, and "a hit with sufficient knockback will defeat them". The fighter who KOs one gets "one point as if they took an opponent's stock". The summoner can KO their own Assist Trophy to deny that point. At most two are on screen at once [primary, fan wiki read in full up to the Ultimate section] (https://www.ssbwiki.com/Assist_Trophy). No design rationale or Sakurai quote is on the page.
- Commentary notes that the point penalty also gives players a reason to leave Assist Trophies alone [secondary] (TV Tropes, search snippet).
- MvC2: assists can't flip out of hitstun the way point characters can, and combos on assists aren't damage-scaled, so hitting point and assist together (a "Happy Birthday") badly hurts the assist [secondary] (https://srk.shib.live/w/Marvel_vs_Capcom_2/System, snippet; Anubis bot-block on fetch).
- Marvel Tokon, EVO 2026 interview: Kazuto Sekine credits lessons from earlier tag games on "how the characters are displayed on the screen for readability". It says nothing about hitting assists [primary] (https://www.shacknews.com/article/149907/marvel-tokon-fighting-souls-evo-2026-developer-interview).
- Dragon Ball FighterZ patch notes tune Z Assist cooldowns up (1.38, C-type) and down (1.40, A and B types). No source ties the cooldown to the assist being hit [secondary] (https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/dragon-ball-fighterz-patch-notes-140, https://media-center.bandainamcoent.eu/games/db-fighterz/DBFZ-Patch138.pdf, snippets).
- BBTag 4Gamer interview (Mori and Ishikawa, 2019): the excerpts cover Ver. 2.0's characters and tournaments, not assists [secondary] (https://blazblue.wiki/wiki/4Gamer_BlazBlue:_Cross_Tag_Battle_Interview_with_Toshimichi_Mori_and_Tatsunori_Ishikawa, search summary).
- Persona 4 Arena's reasoning for Persona Break: not found. Interviews with producer Kazuhisa Wada don't mention it [secondary] (https://megatenwiki.com/wiki/P4A, snippet).

### Candidate additions
- A-019 (updated): add a visible reward for the defender. Breaking a battlemon shows a BREAK! callout and gives the defender a small chunk of super meter, after Smash Ultimate's point for KOing an Assist Trophy [primary]. The summoner can't take the reward away by ending the summon early (Smash's self-KO denial is a party-game quirk that newcomers wouldn't read). No rescore.
- Dropped: unscaled, unescapable combo damage on the battlemon (MvC2's "Happy Birthday"). Battlemons have no health bar, and A-019's hit budget already makes them hittable more simply.
