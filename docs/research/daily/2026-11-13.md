## G-05: HUD and match flow

### Findings
- SF6 Punish Counter: "Any time you hit anyone in recovery, you get punish counter and +4 frames of hit stun" [primary] (https://words.infil.net/w03-sf6beta-p8.html). The same page says SF6 focuses on "readability of the characters over 'realistic' lighting" and that the commentary feature "integrates with the game" [primary].
- SF6 shows "Punish Counter" as text on the side of the screen. A 2024 update added selectable HUD styles, but punish and reversal notifications look the same in every style [secondary] (https://srk.shib.live/w/Street_Fighter_6/HUD, https://dashfight.com/news/street-fighter-6-new-battle-ui-update-leaves-a-lot-to-be-desired-8231).
- Skullgirls dev blog "The HUDsucker Proxy" (2011): at low health the health bar and the damage ("blood") bar were "indistinguishable from one another", so the team requested new bar colors. An intern found the blood bar "doesn't do a good job of informing you how much damage you've dealt during a combo", so they added a temporary combo-damage indicator. Convention players said the horizontal super bar "didn't communicate the level very well" [secondary: search summary; https://skullgirls.com/?p=106 now serves a landing page].
- Guilty Gear Strive's official system page lists six HUD elements. Round Icon: "You lose one when you lose a round." Tension gauge "resets at the start of each round" and rises "through proactive actions like advancing and attacking". The page doesn't describe how a counter hit is shown [primary] (https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/battle/systems/).
- Strive marks big counter hits with slow motion while the attacker keeps moving, plus "COUNTER!" text [secondary] (https://gamesoftrobo.substack.com/p/what-does-guilty-gear-strive-do-right). No developer rationale was found.
- An indie developer's test for HUD size: shrink an element until it is "just barely legible" mid-fight [primary] (https://moldy-skull-games.itch.io/zeitgeist/devlog/577786/3-fighting-game-readability-and-you). This is a rule of thumb, not data.

### Candidate additions
- A-008 (updated): narrowed. The combo count already exists. What's left is PUNISH (a hit on recovery) and COUNTER (a hit on startup) callouts, plus a combo damage readout next to the hit count. Adds the SF6 and Skullgirls evidence.
- A-018 (new): meter rules for the battlemon super. The meter builds from forward play (advancing, attacking, landing hits) and resets each round, after Strive's Tension gauge.
- Dropped: a low-health bar recolor. The red trail drains back to health within about a second (`hud.ts`), so Skullgirls' persistent blood-bar confusion doesn't apply. It can be checked in QA.
- Dropped: commentary or announcer play-by-play. That belongs to step 10 audio, and there's no voice pipeline.

## G-08: Assist and summon design

### Findings
- Skullgirls (Peter Bartholow, Lab Zero, PlayStation Blog 2011): the high/low unblockable is "a common problem in team-based fighters", and "Skullgirls has a short grace period after blocking that will guard against other hit types". Also: "by eliminating the possibility of high/low unblockables, we've been able to allow custom assists". Looping combos can be broken "by hitting any button" [primary] (https://blog.playstation.com/2011/10/27/skullgirls-dev-diary-skullgirls-perfected-pugilism/).
- Sakurai on Smash Brawl's Assist Trophies: they "either attack your opponent or perform a non-targeted attack ... for a short amount of time". He approached them "as something more than a character that attacks your opponent". Picks were judged by "whether or not it would make players happy". On a screen-covering idea: "blocking the screen is something you can't normally get away with in a competitive game like this" [primary] (https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-Super-Smash-Bros-Brawl/Volume-5-Plethora-of-New-Features/2-Assist-Trophies/2-Assist-Trophies-226746.html).
- Smash assist characters are "very powerful ... but also very predictable", so they "can easily be avoided"; in Ultimate nearly all that attack fighters can be damaged [secondary] (https://www.ssbwiki.com/Assist_Trophy).
- Persona 4 Arena: hitting the Persona drains a card gauge. Losing every card causes Persona Break, which disables Persona attacks until the gauge refills (about 10 seconds, version-dependent). The card count varies per character [secondary] (https://www.dustloop.com/w/Gauges_(P4Arena), snippet; Dustloop blocks the fetcher).
- Dragon Ball FighterZ patch 1.40 "Reduced cooldown time needed before next usage" of Z Assists. One commentary says Super Saiyan Vegeta's assist dominated before the first patch [secondary] (https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/dragon-ball/news/dragon-ball-fighterz-patch-notes-140).
- Marvel Tokon's assist cooldowns run longer when the assist is attacked (300F vs 180F) [primary, from the 2026-11-12 note]. So an attackable summon plus a punishment for losing it is a shipped pattern.

### Candidate additions
- A-005 (updated): narrowed to what isn't built. Combo damage scaling, a 2-hit juggle limit and one wall bounce per combo already exist (`src/sim/combat.ts`, spec 012). What's left is a post-block grace period that blocks the other hit level (Skullgirls' rule) so a fighter plus their battlemon can't make an unblockable, and loop detection with a free escape. New primary evidence.
- A-019 (new): the battlemon can be hit. Hitting it ends the summon early and slows the next meter build, so the defender always has counterplay.
- A-015 (updated): Sakurai's "blocking the screen" quote supports the legibility budget.
- Dropped: non-attack summons (buffs and screen effects) as a new addition. The PROMPT domain already does buff and debuff (`game-design.md`, Battlemon domains). Sakurai's "happy" test supports the design as is.
- Dropped: a per-character card gauge (P4A). Too complex for newcomers with one meter. A-019 covers counterplay more simply.
