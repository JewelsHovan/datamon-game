## G-28: Stalemate breakers for simple-input games

### Findings
- GBVS Rising's post-beta changes (Fukuhara: "We had a lot of feedback regarding throws and Raging Strikes"; testers said "Raging Strikes are too strong"). Raging Strike now costs 1 BP, "Cause guard crush and deplete 1 BP to blocking foes", and "Start-up is slower, but reach has been extended". Brave Counter, the answer, uses the same M+H input and can be done while blocking or guard-crushed. Damage taken rises 20% at 1 BP and 50% at 0 BP [primary] (https://gematsu.com/2023/10/granblue-fantasy-versus-rising-details-major-adjustments-to-mechanics-in-response-to-first-open-beta-test).
- GBVS Rising's purpose: "defense is too strong, which makes players prone to stalemate", and both beginners and experts should "use Raging Strikes to break their foe's guard at critical moments" [primary, read 2026-11-16] (https://automaton-media.com/en/interviews/20230901-21299/).
- Guilty Gear Strive's R.I.S.C. gauge "increases as you block, and the higher the gauge, the more damage you take. When the gauge is maxed out, all incoming attacks become counter hits." It's a passive turtle tax, not a move [primary] (https://www.guiltygear.com/ggst/en/battle/systems/). Ishiwatari: "We've intentionally made the R.I.S.C. gauge less visible", and players criticized the UI's visibility [primary] (https://www.arcsystemworks.jp/guiltygear/db/en/volume1.php).
- Strive 2.00 removed Wild Assault, a cheap button that guard-crushed on block, "In alignment with our intention to emphasize a clear shift between offense and defense". With its offensive potential, it "made defense very hard in some situations" [primary, official notes as quoted] (https://dashfight.com/news/guilty-gear-strive-2-0-battle-balance-patch-is-out-and-its-massive-8602). Shacknews calls it "a highly abused mechanic"; the heavy characters' versions were invincible on activation [primary, opinion] (https://www.shacknews.com/article/148678/guilty-gear-strive-2-update-opinion).
- Smash: the shield shrinks with each hit and breaks into a multi-second stun [secondary] (https://www.ssbwiki.com/Shield). Sakurai on Smash 4: "There weren't a lot of people who used the Shield Breaker to break people's shields, so we've now made it much more powerful" [primary] (https://www.siliconera.com/?p=358754, 2013-12-16).
- Infil's glossary: guard crush is "A game mechanic where blocking too many attacks in a short period of time, or a special attack marked with the guard crush..." (truncated); turtling is "Playing overwhelmingly defensively, with as few offensive risks as possible" [primary] (https://glossary.infil.net/json/glossary.json).
- Does blocking dominate newcomer play? Not found. The designer quotes point the other way: newcomers mash. Nakayama: "when they [mash buttons], they just do a lot of whiffs" (SF6 Modern controls). Killian: "you're not going to win a lot of matches sitting back and blocking all the time" (Rising Thunder) [secondary, snippets] (https://gameranx.com/updates/id/397516/article/street-fighter-6-team-discuss-dynamic-control-scheme/; gamesbeat returned 403).
- SF6's Drive Impact rationale from Nakayama or Matsumoto: not found again. Blocking drains the Drive gauge, which taxes turtling [secondary] (https://marcelvaloir.substack.com/p/quarterly-reviews-street-fighter).

### Candidate additions
- A-023 (new): guard strain. Blocking fills a small strain bar under the health bar that drains when you stop blocking. When it's full, the next blocked hit breaks guard into a short stagger. Passive, as with R.I.S.C. and Smash's shield, rather than a dedicated button. Strive removed a cheap guard-crush button, and GBVSR had to slow and charge for its own. The bar stays visible, unlike R.I.S.C.
- Dropped: battlemon supers that break guard by default. Strive's Wild Assault lesson says a cheap guard-crush makes "defense very hard". The super already costs a full meter, and A-005 forbids summon-made unblockables. A domain could opt in later as data.
- Dropped: a GBVSR-style guard-crush button with a matching counter (Raging Strike and Brave Counter). That's two more inputs and a BP economy. The controls table has three buttons, and there's no evidence that newcomer couch play stalls on blocking.

## G-03: Juice values from shipped games

### Findings
- Strive's first beta: Katano heard that "some camera work, such as the effect on counter hit, impacts the game's pace", "player inputs, and visibility". Ishiwatari: the "more daring 3D camera movement" gave some players "motion sickness". The hit counter is big because in older games "it's been difficult to see the number of hits" [primary] (https://www.arcsystemworks.jp/guiltygear/db/en/volume1.php, 2020-06-01).
- Infil's glossary on screen freeze (super flash): "The period directly after a super (or certain other powerful techniques) where the game pauses for dramatic effect" [primary] (https://glossary.infil.net/json/glossary.json).
- KOF XIII: during a super freeze only the player who triggered it can input. The defender can't buffer a reply during the freeze and must input after it ends [secondary] (https://srk.shib.live/w/The_King_of_Fighters_XIII/Game_Elements/Super_Freeze, Anubis-blocked). The SRK glossary says command-grab supers that put their startup before the flash are effectively 0 frames after it, so they can't be reacted to [secondary] (https://srk.shib.live/w/Glossary).
- MUGEN's SuperPause stops the frozen characters' clocks and dims the screen, and the dimming can be turned off [secondary] (https://levelalfaomega.gitbook.io/unity-mugen/unity-mugen/state-controller-reference/superpause).
- Capcom's SF Seminar treats hit stop and block stop together (8/12/16F example). The page returned 403 today, so it stays [secondary] (https://game.capcom.com/cfn/sfv/column/131545?lang=en).
- No primary source with shipped hit-flash frame counts or camera-zoom values was found. The general game-feel blogs carry uncited numbers, so none are used.

### Candidate additions
- A-024 (new): summon freeze. On super activation the screen dims and both fighters freeze for a fixed time as data. Inputs the defender presses during the freeze are dropped, not buffered. The battlemon's first active frame comes a set number of frames after the freeze ends, so the defender can always react to the flash.
- A-008 (updated): the Strive feedback shows a camera or slow-down effect on counter hits hurts pace, inputs and visibility. So PUNISH and COUNTER stay text callouts, with no camera move or slow-down. No rescore.
- Dropped: per-hit-class juice numbers. Spec 004 R5 already sets shake, flash and K.O. slow motion, and no primary source gave better values.
